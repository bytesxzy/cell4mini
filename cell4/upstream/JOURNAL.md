## How to read a rejection

Most candidates are rejected and that is the harness working. `screened out on held-out` means the
candidate solved strictly fewer held-out tasks, so it cannot pass any acceptance clause and the
remaining splits were not run. `not significant` means it gained but the gain is inside the noise
-- on 200 held-out tasks a candidate needs roughly nine wins against at most one loss to clear
p<0.05 on both tests. `regression: lost N` means it broke something a previous champion could do.
